# SpeakConfidently — Market Research Snapshot

**Research date:** 2026-09-21

The market already contains strong products covering AI conversation, pronunciation feedback, roleplay, personalised lessons and mistake-driven practice. Therefore these are capabilities to match, not differentiation by themselves.

## Market conclusion

The following are commoditised enough that SpeakConfidently should not position them as the core differentiator:

- “talk to an AI”
- AI roleplay
- grammar correction
- pronunciation correction
- vocabulary correction
- personalised lessons
- mistake-based lessons
- progress tracking

The strategic product distinction should instead be:

> **A persistent speaking intelligence system that learns the learner's recurring spoken patterns from real conversations and verifies improvement over time.**

The product should be judged by whether it can answer:

**“What changed in the way I actually speak?”**

This research is a directional product input and should be refreshed before commercial launch.
